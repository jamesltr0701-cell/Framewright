## P02 — restrained cancellation

One medium-close shot of Lin carrying genuine excitement about tomorrow. Mei says exactly “The plan is cancelled.” Lin hears it and recognizes what it means; she tries not to burden Mei with her disappointment. Show the change as a process, including a trace of the earlier joy that has not instantly vanished, a delayed understanding, and a restrained withdrawal. Mei remains present and receives Lin's response. Do not add any other words or vocalizations. Do not turn Lin's restraint into resentment, deceit, or accusation.
