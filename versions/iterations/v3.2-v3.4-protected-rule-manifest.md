## Protected mechanisms

The following mechanisms must survive semantically unless the approved decision log names a narrow exception:

1. Director-steered product identity.
2. One workflow and one user entry.
3. Storyboard, Keyframes, and Video Prompt as the only director stages.
4. Exactly one active stage at a time; C06 is a Storyboard-stage delivery exception, not a second stage.
5. Unified Director Intake and its compact question budget.
6. Intake Hard Stop.
7. AUTEUR, APPRENTICE, and SCREENWRITER authority boundaries.
8. Kinetic, observational, and conversational scene grammars.
9. Explicit director locks override inference and adapters.
10. One current approved Production Spine.
11. Start/end state, geography, continuity, and object-state preservation.
12. Entity Token Isolation.
13. Monochrome line-only storyboard planning style.
14. Equal landscape 16:9 storyboard panels on a landscape 16:9 board.
15. Exterior board masthead and no panel-interior paperwork.
16. Structurally limited storyboard asset authority.
17. Storyboard planning-only runtime status until explicit admission.
18. One drawable frozen instant per storyboard panel and keyframe.
19. Shot Design visual sentence and Sequence Shuffle Test.
20. No arbitrary camera variation or camera-angle quotas.
21. Production-critical camera moves state start, path, landing, direction, evidence, and motivation.
22. Executable cinematography carriers instead of empty quality adjectives.
23. Style Survival and Surface Fidelity boundaries.
24. Count, entity, and single-instant locks.
25. Compactness and Compression Safety.
26. Stale-Negative Pass.
27. Generation-Unit Feasibility Gate.
28. No automatic generation-unit split or merge.
29. Semantic timing by default.
30. Continuous-take phases are not cuts.
31. Final Payoff Hold.
32. Performance Vitality.
33. Generated environmental/diegetic sound and no music unless requested.
34. Reference lifecycle and explicit runtime admission.
35. Silent Reference Exclusion.
36. Runtime Cleanliness.
37. Existing prompt output paths; C01, C02, and C06 define the approved boundaries.
38. Validation before save.
39. Prompt-only default, with only C06's one-initial-storyboard-render exception.

## Forbidden implementation shortcuts

- No full-file rewrite of the authoritative specification.
- No wording cleanup outside approved semantic ranges.
- No parallel Production Spine or reference registry.
- No board series.
- No compatibility copy of `uploaded_visual_assets`.
- No automatic retry, variant generation, keyframe generation, or video generation.
- No external generation during implementation tests.
- No historical prompt or project rewrite.
- No modification of untracked project/output directories.
